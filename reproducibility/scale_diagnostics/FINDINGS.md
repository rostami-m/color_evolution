# New diagnostics for the revised paper — 24 November 2026

**The new saved-weight controls support an acute relative-scale effect independently of retraining.** They also separate local induction from global frame sensitivity. They do not establish what caused the separately trained networks' opponent-weight differences.

- Ninety E1 classifiers were evaluated: 30 each trained at 9/d1, 27/d1, and 27/d3. Four probe geometries (9/d1, 9/d3, 27/d1, 27/d3) and both RG/BY axes were tested without training. Thirty E2 center-only classifiers provided the padding negative control. Every native zero-padding test accuracy reproduced the saved value exactly. CPU threads: 2; interop: 1.
- The noise-free simultaneous probe uses physically identical target/surround colors, contrast amplitude ±0.15, and independent target-change or uniform-field calibration. Positive shift means assimilation. Raw signed projection numerators, calibrated shifts, local features, global representations, output logits, finite counts, and calibration denominators are all saved separately.
- For the **same 9-trained weights**, mean local BY shifts were **0.030127 [0.025171, 0.034715]** at 9/d1, **0.009901 [0.008000, 0.011658]** at 27/d1, and **0.030127 [0.025171, 0.034715]** at 27/d3; n = 28 valid seeds throughout. The paired 27/d1-minus-9/d1 difference was **−0.020226 [−0.023064, −0.017157]**. Dilation at 27 restored **+0.020226 [0.017157, 0.023064]**. These descriptive intervals use 10,000 seed-bootstrap draws. Two undefined calibrations remain explicit.
- The same frozen-size attenuation/restoration occurs for the other learned weights. Local BY means at 9/d1, 27/d1, 27/d3 were **0.054228, 0.019444, 0.054228** for 27-fixed-trained weights (n = 28), and **0.033140, 0.010977, 0.033140** for 27-dilated-trained weights (n = 27). See README/summary.json for every CI.
- The copied-weight identity was tested on **46 input images per seed** (32 deterministic random patches plus 14 RG/BY probes). The frozen **float32 atol = rtol = 10⁻⁶ criterion failed for some arrays**; maximum feature, representation, and logit discrepancies were **2.86×10⁻⁵, 8.58×10⁻⁶, and 6.48×10⁻⁵**. Preserving those failures, a separately declared **float64 atol = rtol = 10⁻¹² follow-up passed all 30 networks**, with maxima **3.55×10⁻¹⁵, 5.33×10⁻¹⁵, and 1.78×10⁻¹⁴**. This supports a floating-point accumulation explanation. The exact identity is mathematical, not bitwise float32 equivalence.
- Replacing zeros with **replicate padding** changed test predictions in **2.82%, 1.10%, and 3.37%** of trials for the 9/d1, 27/d1, and 27/d3 source networks. Mean accuracy differences were **+0.500 [−0.197, +1.193]**, **+0.083 [−0.283, +0.480]**, and **+0.360 [−0.413, +1.097] percentage points**, respectively (n = 30). Reflect padding was similar. These are inconclusive accuracy differences, not equivalence tests.
- **Local target BY shifts were exactly unchanged** under either padding replacement for all three E1 groups. Global representations changed. Center-only E2 controls showed **zero changed predictions and zero accuracy difference**, with only float32 roundoff in logits. This matches their lack of a target-to-frame pathway.
- Padding changes after training are **out of distribution**. They do not settle whether outer-frame padding influenced learning. Nor do these probes validate human appearance, correct failed Mach-band signs, or demonstrate distant filling-in. The original normalized opponent-fraction primary interaction remains unchanged and inconclusive.

The protocol was timestamped before execution. The precision follow-up was timestamped after the float32 result and before the float64 check. No tolerance was retrospectively changed. The original run took about 16 seconds excluding interpreter/data loading. `summary.json`, `identity_precision.json`, scripts, per-seed files, and source hashes support reproduction. `scale_diagnostics.pdf` is the figure; `README.md` includes exact commands and all tables.
